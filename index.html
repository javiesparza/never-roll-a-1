Never Roll a 1

A free dice probability game. No wagers, purchases, cash prizes, or rewards.

Roll a virtual six-sided die. A 1 ends your round; ten rolls of 2–6 win it. Each roll is independent. Continue after a win to build a consecutive win streak; a loss resets that streak. One completed round counts as one attempt.
