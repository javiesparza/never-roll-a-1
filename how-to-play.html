How to Play Never Roll a 1

The Basic Rules

Never Roll a 1 is a free, non-wagering dice game: complete ten rolls without a 1. There are no bets or cash prizes. The probability model is a fair six-sided die with independent rolls: 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 are safe, while 1 ends the attempt. Under this model, the chance of completing a fresh attempt is (5/6)10, approximately 16.15%. This is a theoretical rate per attempt, not a measured success rate for the site's players.

Here is how a round unfolds step by step:

  1. Press "Start Game" to begin an attempt and roll the first die immediately. A safe result moves you to 1/10; a 1 ends the attempt on that first roll.
  2. Press "Roll Again" for each subsequent roll. The die animates through several random faces before landing on your result.
  3. Survive each roll by landing on 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6. Any of these results advances your progress bar by one step.
  4. Avoid the 1. If the die lands on 1 at any point during your ten rolls, the round ends immediately and you receive a "Busted" message showing which roll eliminated you.
  5. Reach 10/10 to win the round. Successfully dodging a 1 across all ten rolls triggers the victory screen and increments your win counter.
  6. Use "Start Over" after a loss or "Continue Streak" after a win to reset the board. These buttons prepare the next attempt; press "Start Game" to make its first roll.

Understanding the Progress Bar

The progress bar at the top of the game area provides a visual representation of how far you have advanced in the current round. It fills from left to right with a colorful gradient as you complete each roll. The counter beside it shows your exact progress as a fraction (for example, 7/10 means you have completed seven successful rolls out of the ten required).

The live probability readout shows your remaining chance of winning, given that you have survived so far. With r safe rolls completed, it is (5/6)10−r. After five safe rolls it is approximately 40.19%; after nine it is 83.33%. A completed win is 100%, and an attempt that has already ended on a 1 is 0%.

The die itself does not become safer. At 9/10, the next roll still has a 1/6 chance of being a 1. Your chance of finishing is higher because you have only one obstacle left, not because earlier rolls changed the die.

Intensity Messages

Labels such as "Building Momentum," "Heating Up," and "Danger Zone" mark your progress. They are presentation, not changes to the probability model. A late roll has the same chance of being a 1 as an early roll. You can pause between rolls; speed, timing, and the label do not provide a strategy for improving the theoretical odds.

Win Streaks Explained

A win streak counts consecutive wins within the current UTC day. When you successfully complete a round (reach 10/10), your win streak increments by one. A loss resets it to zero. Midnight UTC also resets the current win streak and streak-attempt counter, even if the page remains open; your saved all-time best is retained.

Before a specified sequence begins, the chance of winning its next two games is approximately 2.61%; for the next three it is 0.4213%. Winning the next five has probability (5/6)50, approximately one in 9,100. These are probabilities for a specified block of games, not the chance of eventually finding a streak somewhere in a longer session. A streak is a record of outcomes, not evidence of a skill advantage.

Stats and Tracking

The game tracks several statistics across three tappable stat cards at the top of the screen. Each card cycles through multiple views when you tap or click it:

Wins Card: Cycles between "Today's Wins" (wins since midnight UTC) and "All-Time Wins" (the total in this browser's saved record).
Streak Card: Cycles between "Win Streak" (your current consecutive wins), "Today's Best" (longest streak achieved today), and "All-Time Best" (your longest streak ever).
Attempts Card: Cycles between "Today's Attempts," "Streak Attempts," and "All-Time Attempts." Attempt counters update when a round ends in a win or loss. Starting or resetting the board does not increment them; abandoned rounds are not included.

Personal statistics are saved in this browser's local storage when it is available. Clearing site data, using another browser, or ending a private-browsing session can remove or separate that record. Signing in does not turn all local statistics into a cross-device history. Today's wins, attempts, and best streak reset at midnight UTC, matching the daily leaderboard's date boundary. All-time totals and best streak persist. Midnight does not clear an active round's roll progress; its result counts on the UTC date when it finishes.

Your displayed success rate describes recorded results; it is not a forecast. For example, two wins in ten completed attempts is 20%, but the next fresh attempt still has a theoretical win chance of approximately 16.15%. A round still in progress is not included in that calculation.

The Leaderboard

The optional leaderboard displays submitted scores. Sign in with Google and choose a display name to participate. Wins are saved automatically while you are signed in and have a display name, subject to a working connection. The leaderboard has two tabs:

  • Daily: Shows the top 10 scores for today, ranked by highest win streak with fewest attempts as a tiebreaker.
  • All-Time: Shows the top 25 scores across the entire history of the game.

Use the account controls to sign in before playing if you want wins submitted automatically. A better qualifying score updates your leaderboard entry; a later loss does not erase that saved best. A public leaderboard is a selection of submitted high scores, not a representative sample from which to estimate the game's win probability.

Sharing Your Results

After winning a round, use "Share Your Victory" to share a text summary. Where supported, the browser opens your device's share sheet; otherwise the game attempts to copy the text to your clipboard. Support and permissions vary by browser. Sharing does not change your score or the next roll.

Review the text before posting it. Sharing is optional, and you can play without an account or a public score.

Tips for New Players

  • Expect variation. A fresh attempt has approximately an 83.85% chance of ending on a 1. A loss is not a sign that you played incorrectly.
  • Do not chase a result. Earlier losses do not make a win due. Stop whenever you like; there is no need to complete a streak.
  • Compare the right quantities. The remaining-win readout concerns the current surviving attempt; your recorded success rate concerns earlier outcomes.
  • Check out the math. Visit our Probability Guide to understand why the odds work the way they do.
