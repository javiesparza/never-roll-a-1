Contact Us

Have questions, feedback, or found a bug? Contact the maintainer of javiesparza/never-roll-a-1. This is an independent project; no response or resolution time is guaranteed. Before reaching out, you may find your answer in our FAQ.

Email

contact@neverrollone.com

Use a descriptive subject for general questions or private requests. If email is unavailable, you can raise a non-sensitive issue in the project's public issue tracker. Do not post email addresses, account identifiers, or other private information there.

Report a Bug

Include your browser name and version, device type, the page address, steps to reproduce the problem, and what happened versus what you expected. Screenshots can help, but remove private information first. Reports are handled as maintainer availability permits.

Feature Requests

Tell us what you would like to see and how it would support a simple, educational probability game. Suggestions do not guarantee implementation.

Report a Leaderboard Entry

Email a leaderboard report with the Daily or All-Time tab, the UTC date (especially for Daily entries), the exact display name, and the visible streak and attempts. Explain the suspected issue, such as an offensive name, impersonation, or a manipulated score. Include a screenshot or other relevant details without exposing private information.

The operator's procedure is manual: review the report and available records, then remove or correct an entry when a violation is established. Repeated abuse may lead to manual account restrictions or removal. Reports are not automatically verified, and no automated moderation or guaranteed review time is provided. A high score alone is not proof of cheating.

Request Account or Score Deletion

Email a data deletion request stating whether you want the Firebase Authentication account, Firestore user profile, daily scores, all-time scores, or all of these removed. You can also ask about account-linked moderation records and any applicable retention duties. Include your leaderboard display name and relevant UTC dates; use the account's email address if possible. The maintainer may need to verify control of the account before removing records. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or tokens.

Clearing browser storage only deletes local stats and session data; signing out ends your session. Neither action deletes remote account or leaderboard records. See the privacy choices for details.

Legal & Privacy

For questions about your data, privacy rights, or our terms, please see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. If you have a specific legal inquiry, reach out via email and include "Legal" in the subject line.
