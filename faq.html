Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dice roll truly random?

The game uses JavaScript's Math.random(), a browser-provided pseudorandom generator, and maps its output to six faces. It does not adjust the odds based on previous wins or losses. This is intended to model independent fair-die rolls for a probability game, not to provide cryptographic randomness or independently certified outcomes.

What are my actual odds of winning?

For independent fair-die rolls, the chance of winning one attempt is exactly (5/6)^10, or approximately 16.15%. That is about 1 in 6.2, not a promise of a win every six attempts. Each roll has a 5 out of 6 chance of being safe; multiply those probabilities across ten rolls. For a deeper explanation, visit our Probability Guide.

I keep losing. Is the game rigged?

The game does not change the odds to make you lose. Under the independent fair-die model, an attempt has about an 83.85% chance of failure. The probability of zero wins in ten complete attempts is (1 − (5/6)^10)^10 ≈ 17.18%. This is a theoretical probability, not a measured player statistic. Previous losses do not make your next attempt more likely to win, and no number of attempts guarantees success.

How does the leaderboard work?

The leaderboard shows Daily (top 10) and All-Time (top 25) entries, ranked by win streak and then fewer attempts. Daily entries use today's UTC date. While signed in with a leaderboard display name, a win automatically attempts to save a qualifying personal-best score; there is no separate opt-in for each win. Other players' results can still change your ranking. These are client-submitted scores, not independently verified records. To flag an entry, use the leaderboard reporting instructions.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Dice rolling and local statistics work without an account, and anyone can view the public leaderboard. Google sign-in and a display name are needed to submit scores. Stay signed out if you do not want your wins automatically submitted to the leaderboard.

Does the game work on mobile devices?

The layout is designed for phones, tablets, and desktops using a modern browser. Browser settings can affect storage, Google sign-in, and sharing support. If something does not work on your device, send the browser and device details through the contact page.

What data is local, and what becomes public?

Browser localStorage holds your game statistics. If you use Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication handles profile information, email, provider identifiers, an account UID, and your auth session. Firestore stores your chosen display name and profile creation timestamp. While signed in with a display name, winning automatically submits qualifying scores. Daily and all-time score records are publicly readable and contain your account-linked user ID, display name, streak, attempts, and timestamp; daily records also include the UTC date. The ID is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Choose a non-identifying display name and read the Privacy Policy.

Why did my stats reset?

Daily wins, daily attempts, and the daily leaderboard use midnight UTC, not your local midnight. The game applies the new day when it next checks the date. Your active win streak carries across UTC midnight; a loss, not a date change, resets it. All-time stats are preserved across days when browser storage is available. A new daily view does not delete older cloud score records. If all local stats disappeared, browser storage may have been cleared, or you may be using a different browser, device, or private window. Local stats do not sync between devices, and signing in does not restore them from leaderboard records.

Attempts count completed rounds, including wins, rather than individual die rolls. A loss resets the win streak and starts a new streak-attempt sequence with that loss counted as its first attempt.

Can I play offline?

After the game has fully loaded while online, dice rolling and local stats can continue in the open page without a connection, provided browser storage is available. Sign-in, loading leaderboard entries, and saving cloud scores require internet access. Offline wins are not guaranteed to reach the leaderboard later. Reopening or reloading the site offline is not supported as an offline-install feature. Ads are currently disabled.

How can I suggest a new feature?

Visit our Contact page to send suggestions, bug reports, or general feedback to the project maintainer. Explain the idea and how it would help the game. There is no guaranteed response time or promise that a feature will be implemented.

Is this a gambling game?

No money is wagered, no purchases are required, and no monetary prizes are offered. This is a free entertainment and probability-learning game intended for a general audience, not specifically directed to children. Visitors below the applicable age for these account services in their jurisdiction should not use Google sign-in or submit leaderboard entries. See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

How do I remove my data?

Clear this site's browser storage to remove local statistics. Signing out ends the app's signed-in session but does not remove local stats or cloud records. To request deletion of the Firebase Authentication account, Firestore user profile, and daily or all-time scores, follow the data deletion instructions. Remote deletion requires manual handling and may require account verification.

Does the site use ads or Google Analytics?

Ads are disabled and no AdSense ad requests are sent. Google Analytics is not configured. Hosting and Firebase may still process service requests as described in the Privacy Policy. Any future advertising launch requires an updated policy, validated ad setup, and a Google-certified CMP where applicable, with verified consent and withdrawal choices.
