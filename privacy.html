Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

The Project and This Policy

Never Roll a 1 is an independent browser game maintained through javiesparza/never-roll-a-1 on GitHub. This identifies the public project and maintainer account, not a verified legal operator identity. Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent through the contact page.

You can play without signing in. Local game statistics and optional cloud account and leaderboard records are different kinds of data. Signing in enables automatic score submission on wins once a leaderboard display name is set; you do not separately opt in for each win.

Browser Storage

  • Local game statistics: localStorage stores wins, attempts, streaks, best streaks, and the daily date marker so statistics can survive page refreshes in the same browser. These local statistics do not sync between devices.
  • Authentication session: Firebase Authentication uses browser storage to maintain sign-in state. Google sign-in may also use cookies or other storage under Google's policies.
  • Browser storage can be unavailable or cleared by your browser, private browsing settings, or you. Keeping local stats is not a backup guarantee.

Google Sign-In and Firebase Records

Firebase Authentication handles Google sign-in, including account profile information (such as name and profile image), email address, provider identifiers, the Firebase user ID (UID), and authentication session information. The application does not receive your Google password.

  • Firestore users: a document keyed by the account UID stores the chosen displayName and createdAt timestamp for the leaderboard profile. The repository's access rules restrict browser reads of this profile record to its signed-in owner; the name is also included in public score records.
  • Firestore dailyScores and allTimeScores: score records contain userId, displayName, streak, attempts, and timestamp. Daily records also contain date, using a UTC day.
  • Moderation records: the manual abuse-handling procedure uses administrator-managed blockedUsers records keyed by UID to prevent further profile and score writes. Repository rules deny browser access to these records. They are not a public leaderboard field or an automated content-moderation system.
  • The UID is a pseudonymous account identifier linked to authentication, not anonymous data. Although score records do not include your email address or Google profile image, Firebase Authentication processes those account details.

These services support sign-in, persistent display names, and score comparison. A win while signed in with a display name automatically attempts to save a qualifying personal-best score. Internet or service failures can prevent saving.

The repository includes access rules, but their deployment and the live Firebase configuration have not been verified. Publishing the website alone does not deploy those rules. These descriptions of intended restrictions are not a certification of live access controls.

What Is Public

The daily and all-time score collections are publicly readable, including account-linked userId values, names, results, and timestamps. This is broader than the top entries currently displayed in the leaderboard. Others may copy public records. Choose a display name that does not reveal personal information, and do not submit someone else's private information.

Midnight UTC starts a new daily view and resets daily wins and attempts, while an active win streak carries over. It does not delete previous daily records from Firestore. All-time records are also separate from local browser statistics.

Service Providers and Hosting

  • Google/Firebase: the game loads Firebase resources and contacts its services for authentication and leaderboard functionality. Those requests may occur when viewing the game or public leaderboard, even without signing in. See Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting: the hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information, and timestamps to deliver and protect the site. This policy does not assert a verified log-retention period.
  • Contact: if you email the maintainer, your address, message, and any attachments are used to handle that request. GitHub issues are public and subject to GitHub's policies; do not use them to share private account details.

Advertising and Analytics Are Not Enabled

Advertising is currently disabled. The site does not load AdSense or send AdSense ad requests. Google Analytics is not configured, and we do not claim to collect anonymous analytics. Hosting and Firebase service requests described above are separate from advertising or Google Analytics.

Any future advertising launch requires an updated privacy policy, validation of the advertising account and setup, and a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) in applicable jurisdictions. Consent, refusal, and withdrawal choices must be implemented and verified before ads are enabled. There are no placeholder consent controls on this site, and this policy is not a consent mechanism or a claim of legal compliance.

Your Choices and Deletion

  • Play signed out: no account is needed for dice rolling or local stats. Sign out before playing if you do not want subsequent wins automatically submitted.
  • Delete local stats: clear this site's localStorage or site data in your browser settings. Clearing all site data may also clear the local authentication session. This does not remove Firebase Authentication, user profile, or score records.
  • Sign out: this ends the app's signed-in session; it does not delete local game statistics or remote records.
  • Request remote deletion: use the account and score deletion contact instructions to request removal of the Firebase Authentication account, Firestore users document, dailyScores, and allTimeScores. Specify all records you want removed. This is a manual request, not an in-game delete-account feature; verification of account control may be needed.

There is no fixed automatic retention or deletion schedule promised here. Local data remains until cleared or evicted by the browser; cloud records may remain until manually removed. Ask about any account-linked moderation records in a deletion request; their handling requires review of applicable retention duties rather than a promise of unconditional erasure. Provider logs and backups are governed by provider settings and policies; their retention has not been verified for this deployment. Removing records from this project cannot recall copies already made by others from the public leaderboard.

General Audience and Younger Visitors

The game is intended for a general audience, not specifically directed to children. If you are below the applicable age for using these account services in your jurisdiction, do not use Google sign-in or submit leaderboard entries. A parent or guardian can contact the maintainer about a child's account or score data. This guidance is not an age-verification system and does not by itself establish compliance with children's privacy requirements.
